FAQ

Questions, answered

Can’t see your question? Send me a message and I’ll get back to you.

How much does a website cost?

Most small business websites cost between £750 and £3,500, depending on the number of pages and features. After a free call you’ll get a fixed quote, before any work starts.

How long will it take?

A one-page site usually takes 1 to 2 weeks, and a full business website 3 to 4 weeks. The biggest factor is how quickly content and feedback come back.

Do I need to write the content?

You know your business best, so I’ll send a short questionnaire about your services and customers. I’ll then help turn your answers into clear, persuasive website copy.

Can I update the website myself?

Yes. Business and Growth sites include a simple editor for your text, images and menus, and I’ll show you how to use it. If you’d rather not, a care plan covers edits for you.

Will my website show up on Google?

Every site is built with SEO foundations: fast loading, proper page titles and descriptions, a sitemap and Google Search Console set-up. Rankings take time, but you’ll start in the strongest position.

What about hosting and my domain?

I’ll help you register a domain if you don’t have one and set up fast, secure hosting. The domain is always registered in your name.

What happens after launch?

You get 30 days of free fixes after launch. After that you can join a monthly care plan, or just get in touch whenever you need changes.
