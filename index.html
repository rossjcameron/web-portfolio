(03) Process

From first call to launch day.

A clear, four-step process so you always know what is happening and what comes next.

  1. 01

    Discovery call

    A free 30-minute call about your business, your customers and what the site needs to achieve. You get a fixed quote within 48 hours.

    Week 1
  2. 02

    Design

    Wireframes, then full designs you can click through. Two rounds of revisions included, so you see exactly what you are getting before any code is written.

    Weeks 1–2
  3. 03

    Build

    Hand-coded, responsive and fast. Accessible by default and tested on real phones, not just a desktop browser.

    Weeks 2–4
  4. 04

    Launch & care

    I set up your domain, hosting and analytics, then stay on hand with an optional monthly care plan for updates and edits.

    Launch day
